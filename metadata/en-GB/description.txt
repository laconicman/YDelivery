Send a parcel the way you would drop a pin. YDelivery is a delivery app for people and small businesses — order from a map, see the price before you commit, hear when the courier arrives, share the tracking, repeat last week's run in one tap.

Delivery providers plug in behind one boundary. The first integration is Yandex Delivery's Express (B2B Cargo) API — unofficial, on your business account's terms.

• Order from a map, not from coordinates — pick points visually, use saved places and recents, or paste a link.
• The price before you commit — every tariff shown up front, on the review sheet, before the tap.
• Every order you sent, on every device you own — synced through your private iCloud, searchable by address and your own fields.
• A page you never refresh — a notification on every status change, and a Live Activity that follows the delivery on your Lock Screen.
• Share a delivery without sharing your account — a recipient or colleague follows the order, chats per order, confirms receipt — no provider login needed for them.
• Plans change — cancel with the fee shown before you tap.
• The sender's library — parcel templates, pinned places, and the fields your paperwork needs, ready for the next order.

Requires a Yandex Delivery business account and its API token — your cabinet, at business terms. Delivery is paid to Yandex on your business account.
