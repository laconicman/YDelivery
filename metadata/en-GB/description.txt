YDelivery is an unofficial client for Yandex Delivery business accounts — the delivery service's Express API in your pocket, with no backend and no integrator.

Place orders from a map instead of coordinates: pick points visually, use saved places and recents, or paste a link. See the price before you commit — every tariff shown up front — and repeat last week's run in one tap.

Every order you sent stays on every device you own, synced through your private iCloud, searchable by address and your own fields. The app keeps itself updated: a notification on every status change and a Live Activity that follows the delivery on your Lock Screen — no page to refresh.

Share a delivery without sharing your account: let a recipient or a colleague follow the order, chat per order, confirm receipt — no Yandex login needed for them. And if plans change, cancel with the fee shown before the tap.

Requires a Yandex Delivery business account and its API token — your cabinet, at business terms. Delivery is paid to Yandex on your business account. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Yandex.
