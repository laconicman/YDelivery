Round 1 · Board 1 · draft screen

Two shells for the route, price in both positions

Russian-first. Both shells carry the same parts from §1.4 — from/to rows with colored end-dots, drag handles, swap, a tariff strip of provider data, an estimate bar labelled as an estimate. They differ in where the map sits and when price is allowed to arrive. Contacts are a collapsed row under each point (§1.5 Q3), so the picker stays a picker.

Pins follow §2.2: role reads from shape and glyph first — round dot for start, teardrop for end — with the color pair moved to Okabe-Ito green #1E8E3E / vermillion #D55E00. Options: 1a map-first, 1b fixed card.

1a

Map-first taxi shell

price in the sheet · Uber/Bolt position

The sheet is the whole conversation and grows: route card → estimate → tariff strip when offers land. Pricing latency costs nothing because the strip appears below what you were already reading. The cost is that at accessibility sizes the route is never fully visible without expanding the sheet.

«Проезд» re-phrased — fixed card

map on top · route card and tariff strip below · price on the card, 2GIS position

The route card never moves and is fully readable without gestures — the safer shell at accessibility sizes, and the one the repo screenshots argue for. Price must then arrive into a strip that is already on screen, so the strip needs an honest waiting state and a failure state that doesn't collapse the layout.
