Round 3 · system surfaces & reuse

The app is mostly used while it's closed

This is the round the earlier boards implied but didn't draw. Ordering takes ninety seconds; waiting takes forty minutes, and during those forty minutes the sender is serving a customer, packing the next parcel, or driving. Every minute of that is spent on the Lock Screen, in the Dynamic Island, or in a notification — never in the app. A delivery has a definite start, a definite end and a monotonic status, which is exactly the shape a Live Activity is for.

5a Live Activity & Dynamic Island · 5b widgets · 5c notifications · 5d intents, Spotlight & sharing · 5e the shared component set · 5f HIG audit.

5a

Live Activity — one glance, one action

Content rule, in priority order: where is it now, when will it be there, who to call if something's wrong. Your own order number is the identifier shown, not the vendor's claim id — this is the one place the sender's vocabulary must win, because they're matching it against a customer chat. The dashed remainder of the route is the only graphic; a live map in a Live Activity is a battery bill for information the ETA already gives.

5b

Widgets — one waiting, one working

Two jobs only. Waiting: the active delivery, so the Home Screen answers "where is it" without a tap. Working: repeat a saved route, so tomorrow's warehouse run starts from the Home Screen — an App Intent that opens the draft pre-filled, never one that orders silently. Money must not move from a widget tap.

5c

Notifications — earn each one

While a Live Activity is on screen, routine progress needs no push — that's the whole point of it. Push only for what the sender must act on: courier assigned, courier at the door, delivered, and the two failures. Every notification carries the order number in the title, because it will be read next to a customer's message. Actions come from the notification, not from opening the app.

5d

Ways in, and one way out

The Share Sheet is the answer to a question board 2 solved awkwardly: senders receive addresses in chats. Share a Maps place or a plain address into the app and it opens a draft with that point filled — better than any paste affordance, because it's where the address already is. Sharing outward matters just as much: the customer wants to know where their parcel is, and today that's a manual retyping.

App Intents · три, не тридцать
«Где моя доставка» — Siri and Spotlight read the status aloud; no UI needed.
«Повторить доставку в …» — opens a pre-filled draft. Parameterised by saved place, so it works from a Shortcut.
«Отправить по заказу …» — takes the sender's own order number, which is the identifier they think in.
Spotlight. Index completed orders by custom-field values via CSSearchableItem: typing «4417» in Spotlight finds the delivery. That is the payoff of 4b that lives outside the app entirely.
5e

The shared set — five components, four targets

Widgets and Live Activities can't import the app target, so anything shared must live in a framework or local package below both. The test for membership: does it render from plain values with no controller, no network, no environment? Five things pass, and they're the five that appear on every surface above. Everything else stays in the app.

ComponentRenders fromUsed by
StatusChip status enum → colour, glyph, text. Never colour alone app · widget · activity · notification
RouteLine points + progress → dots, teardrop, solid/dashed segments app · widget · activity · notification
PointBadge role + index → the 2c shape/glyph pair at three sizes app · widget · notification
ETALabel date + confidence → «в 9:41», «~14 мин», or «обычно 3–7 мин» when there is no estimate app · widget · activity
OrderIdentity custom fields → the sender's own order number, formatted once app · widget · activity · notification · Spotlight
Sizing, not styling

Each takes a size of compact / regular / expanded rather than reading Dynamic Type directly — a widget can't grow, so it must shrink content instead. Same component, three densities, one place to change them.

Colour lives with them

The asset catalog moves into the same package, otherwise the widget's green drifts from the app's. Widgets render on unpredictable backgrounds, so every token needs a light and dark value and none may rely on the app's material.

One store, one truth

Widgets read the order store directly, so it belongs in an App Group from day one. Retrofitting that after Keychain and history exist is the expensive version of this decision.

5f

HIG audit — what we're getting wrong

Read against the guidelines rather than against my own boards. Six findings, ordered by how much they'd cost to fix later.

1
The tab bar is wrong for this app — confirmed, and already avoided in the demo

«New Delivery» is an action, and tabs are for peer locations, not verbs. It also means a tab switch destroys the draft. Two tabs — Доставки, Настройки — with a prominent «Новая доставка» on the list, presenting the flow modally: the compose idiom, and the draft's lifetime becomes legible. The regular-width version of the same structure is 6c, where the list becomes a sidebar and ⌘N replaces the button.

2
Ordering is irreversible and nothing acknowledges that

Money moves and a courier is dispatched. It needs a confirmation step that restates route, price and tariff — and given how .confirmationDialog reads on a small screen, a review sheet is the honest form. It's also the natural home for the required-custom-field check from 4b.

3
Phone, address and name fields aren't declaring themselves

Every contact field should carry textContentType plus the matching keyboard type — .phonePad for phone, .numberPad for flat and floor, .decimalPad for weight and cost — so autofill and the right keys arrive together. Addresses should offer the Contacts picker rather than only typing. A sender entering a customer's details is always copying from somewhere: the same row also carries the camera route from 6a, whose implementation can be deferred without leaving a hole in the UI, since manual entry stays a complete path.

4
Offline is a normal condition, not an error

Basements, lifts, loading bays. History and saved places must be fully readable offline; only pricing and ordering need the network, and they should say which one is missing rather than failing generically. A draft must survive being written offline.

5
The map needs a VoiceOver equivalent, not a label

Callouts in 4a are a shortcut for people who think in maps; the same content must be reachable through the row's disclosure. Confirm-pin needs a spoken address plus a "point moved" announcement, and the tariff strip needs each card as its own element with limits in the label — otherwise choosing a tariff is guesswork.

6
Small wins, and one that isn't small

Swipe actions on history rows (повторить, наоборот, удалить) — the standard idiom for a list of records. Context menu on a saved place. .searchable on Доставки, since 4b made orders findable. The regular-width layout is the one that isn't small: designed as iPad with a keyboard it yields the Mac app nearly for free, and it's drawn in 6c with its shortcut set.
